About Data Curator

Data Curator is a biweekly newsletter at the intersection of Technology, Data, AI and Humanity.

Written by Hillel Adler — a product and data professional with over 20 years of diverse experience spanning data systems, AI, and product strategy. Based in Tel Aviv.

The newsletter explores the deeper questions that sit at the fault lines where technology meets society, data meets ethics, and data meets meaning. From the history of the machine gun to neuro-symbolic AI, from data tradecraft to the cultural assumptions embedded in large language models.

What to expect

  • Deep dives into AI, data, and technology
  • Book reviews that connect historical patterns to current moments
  • Multi-part series exploring complex topics
  • No hot takes. No hype. Just careful thinking.
